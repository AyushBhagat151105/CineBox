Privacy & Data Protection Policy

1. Zero Telemetry & No Tracking

CineBox is built on a strict privacy-first foundation. The application contains zero third-party tracking libraries, zero analytic SDKs, zero session trackers, and zero behavioral profiling. We do not track what you search, what you watch, or how often you use the app.

2. No Central Accounts or Identity

CineBox requires no account creation, email registration, phone number verification, or payment credentials. Your watch history, bookmarks, and settings are saved exclusively on your local device storage.

3. Direct Client-to-Provider Communications

When CineBox indexes search queries or launches streams in VLC, the network requests are executed directly from your local client device to the public third-party provider or CDN. No intermediate CineBox proxy or relay server intercepts or logs your network traffic.

4. In-App Updates

The in-app update checker communicates directly with the official GitHub Releases API (`api.github.com`) using standard HTTPS requests to check for new version tags and download published APK assets. No personal identifiable information is transmitted during update checks.
